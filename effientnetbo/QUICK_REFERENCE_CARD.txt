## 🔧 Key Changes Made

### Enhanced Training (train_hydenet_improved.py)
- Epochs: 20 → 50
- Batch Size: 16 → 32
- Augmentation: 3 → 6 techniques
- Learning Rate: Fixed → Scheduled

### Advanced Model (train_hydenet_advanced.py)
- Model: EfficientNet-B0 → B2
- Input: 224×224 → 260×260
- Dense Layers: 1 → 3
- Training: 1-phase → 2-phase with fine-tuning

## 🎓 Understanding the Numbers

**Accuracy**: Percentage of correct predictions
- 75% = 3 out of 4 gestures correct (acceptable)
- 90% = 9 out of 10 gestures correct (good)
- 95% = 19 out of 20 gestures correct (excellent)

**Per-Class Accuracy**: For each gesture type
- If gesture A = 95% but B = 60%, you have an imbalance
- Collect more data for the weak class

**Confidence Score**: Model's certainty (0.0 to 1.0)
- > 0.7 = High confidence (usually correct)
- 0.5-0.7 = Medium confidence (risky)
- < 0.5 = Low confidence (likely wrong)

## 💡 Pro Tips

**Tip 1: Data > Model**
- 80% of improvement comes from better data
- 20% comes from better training/architecture

**Tip 2: Diversity is Key**
- Different angles, lighting, distances
- Different users if possible
- Different backgrounds

**Tip 3: Start Simple**
- Train B0 first (fast)
- Then try B2 (better but slower)
- Use B0 for deployment unless B2 is necessary

**Tip 4: Monitor Carefully**
- Check confusion matrix regularly
- Focus data collection on weak gestures
- Track accuracy progression over time

**Tip 5: Save Your Models**
- Keep backup of good models
- Name them with accuracy: `model_92pct_acc.h5`
- Never delete training scripts
